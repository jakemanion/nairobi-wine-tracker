# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 17 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **WineDiviner: Nairobi** (“WineDiviner”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and related services (the “Service”).

WineDiviner helps people discover and track wines available in Nairobi. This document is provided for transparency and does not constitute legal advice. If you have questions, contact us using the details below.

## 1. Who we are

WineDiviner: Nairobi is operated as a consumer wine discovery and personal tracking tool. The Service may display publicly available product information (such as producer, wine name, region, ratings, and store prices) and allow registered users to save personal preferences.

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Account information
When you create an account, we collect:

- Email address
- Authentication credentials (handled by our authentication provider; we do not store your raw password)
- Optional profile details such as display name or username

### 2.2 Personal wine preferences
If you use personal features, we may store:

- Bookmark / want-to-try selections
- Shortlist selections
- Tried / buy-again preferences
- Personal notes
- Hide preferences
- Shared list settings you choose to publish

### 2.3 Technical and usage information
We may automatically process limited technical data needed to run the Service, such as:

- Session and authentication cookies
- Browser type and basic device information
- Pages or features you access
- Approximate timestamps of activity

### 2.4 Information from third parties
Wine catalogue data (for example Vivino ratings or retailer listings) may be sourced from third-party sites or partners. That information relates to products, not to you personally, unless you choose to associate it with your account preferences.

## 3. How we use information

We use information to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve the Service
- Authenticate accounts and keep them secure
- Save your wine preferences and shared lists
- Respond to support requests
- Detect abuse, fraud, or technical issues
- Comply with applicable law

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## 4. Legal bases (where applicable)

Where data-protection laws require a legal basis, we generally process personal data to:

- Perform our contract with you (providing the Service)
- Pursue legitimate interests (security, product improvement, preventing misuse)
- Comply with legal obligations
- Obtain your consent where required (for example, optional communications)

## 5. Sharing of information

We may share information with:

- **Service providers** that help us operate the Service (for example hosting, authentication, and database providers such as Supabase)
- **Professional advisers** or authorities when required by law
- **Other users**, only when you intentionally create a public shared list. Shared lists are designed to show curated wine catalogue information, not your private notes or ratings

We require processors to protect personal data appropriately.

## 6. Shared lists

If you generate a shared list link, anyone with that link may view the wines included according to your selected collections. You control which collections are included and can regenerate the link. Do not share a link if you do not want others to see those wines.

## 7. Cookies and similar technologies

We use essential cookies and similar storage (including browser local storage) to:

- Keep you signed in
- Remember age-gate confirmation
- Remember certain interface preferences (such as usage tips)

See our [Cookie Policy](/cookies) for more detail.

## 8. Data retention

We retain account and preference data while your account remains active and as needed to provide the Service. You may request deletion of your account data subject to legal retention requirements.

## 9. Security

We use industry-standard measures appropriate to the nature of the Service, including encrypted transport (HTTPS) and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## 10. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where required, we take steps intended to ensure appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

## 11. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your data
- Object to or restrict certain processing
- Export data in a portable format
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

## 12. Children’s privacy

The Service is intended only for people of legal drinking age in their country of residence. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone who is not of legal drinking age.

## 13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use of the Service after changes means you accept the updated policy, except where applicable law requires otherwise.
