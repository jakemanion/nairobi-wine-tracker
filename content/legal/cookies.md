# Cookie Policy

**Last updated:** 21 October 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how **WineDiviner: Nairobi** (“WineDiviner”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on the Service.

For broader information about personal data, see our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include local storage and session storage in your browser.

## 2. How we use cookies and similar technologies

WineDiviner currently uses cookies and local storage primarily for **essential** operation of the Service:

| Technology | Purpose | Typical duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Authentication / session cookies | Keep you signed in securely via our auth provider | Session or provider-defined |
| Age-gate confirmation (local storage) | Remember that you confirmed you are of legal drinking age | Until cleared by you or your browser |
| Analytics consent choice (local storage) | Remember whether you accepted or declined optional analytics | Until cleared by you or your browser |
| PostHog analytics cookies / local storage | Product analytics (page views, feature usage, error reporting) after you accept analytics | Until cleared by you or your browser, or as defined by PostHog |
| Usage tips preference (local storage) | Remember whether you want interface tips shown | Until cleared by you or your browser |
| Welcome banner preference (local storage) | Remember if you dismissed the welcome message | Until cleared by you or your browser |

These technologies are used to provide the Service you request, maintain security, and remember basic interface choices.

## 3. Analytics, advertising, and marketing cookies

WineDiviner uses **PostHog** (EU Cloud) for optional product analytics so we can understand how the Service is used and improve it. Analytics cookies / storage are loaded **only if you choose “Accept analytics”** on the cookie banner.

When enabled, PostHog may:

- Distinguish anonymous visits from signed-in sessions
- Measure page views and feature usage
- Support error reporting

We identify signed-in users with an opaque account ID only. We do **not** send your email address or password to PostHog as an analytics identifier.

WineDiviner does **not** use advertising or marketing cookies. Choosing **Essential only** keeps the Service working without analytics cookies.

## 4. Managing cookies

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through settings. If you block essential cookies, parts of the Service (including sign-in) may not work.

To change your analytics choice later, clear this site’s local storage (or site data) for WineDiviner and reload the page — the cookie banner will appear again.

You can usually clear local storage from your browser’s site settings for this domain.

## 5. Third-party cookies

If you follow links to retailers, Vivino, or other external sites, those sites may set their own cookies. This Cookie Policy does not cover third-party websites.

## 6. Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.
