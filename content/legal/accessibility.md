# Accessibility Statement

**Last updated:** 17 July 2026

**WineDiviner: Nairobi** is committed to making our website usable by as many people as possible, including people with disabilities.

This statement describes our accessibility goals, known limitations, and how to contact us if you encounter barriers.

## 1. Our commitment

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA where reasonably practicable for a product of this scale.

We design and build with attention to:

- Readable typography and colour contrast
- Keyboard-operable controls where feasible
- Clear labels and focus states for interactive elements
- Responsive layouts that work on mobile and desktop
- Meaningful text alternatives for important interactive controls

## 2. Conformance status

WineDiviner is a continuously evolving product. Parts of the Service may not yet fully meet WCAG 2.2 AA.

We treat accessibility as an ongoing improvement process rather than a one-time checklist.

## 3. Measures we take

- Prefer semantic HTML for headings, buttons, links, and forms
- Provide accessible names for icon-only controls where practical
- Support responsive layouts for smaller screens
- Avoid conveying essential information by colour alone where we can
- Test key flows during development (browse, search, sign-in, personal lists)

## 4. Known limitations

You may currently experience barriers in areas such as:

- Complex filter controls and custom dropdowns
- Image-heavy wine cards or bottle previews
- Dense information layouts on smaller screens
- Overlay dialogs (for example share lists or age verification)
- Third-party embedded content or outbound retailer pages beyond our control

We intend to improve these areas over time.

## 5. Compatibility

The Service is designed to work with recent versions of major browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge) on common desktop and mobile devices.

Assistive technology support may vary depending on browser and operating system combinations.

## 6. Feedback

If you find an accessibility barrier, please tell us:

**WineDiviner: Nairobi**  
Email: accessibility@winediviner.example

Please include:

- The page URL
- A description of the problem
- Your browser and assistive technology (if relevant)

We will review feedback and aim to respond within a reasonable time.

Replace this contact address with your live support email before public launch.

## 7. Formal complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may have rights to raise concerns with a relevant equality or consumer authority in your jurisdiction.

## 8. Preparation of this statement

This statement was prepared on 17 July 2026 based on an internal review of the Service’s main user flows.
